Project Overview:
My project creates a school cafeteria simulation, a program designed to simulate 
students moving through different food-station lines in a school cafeteria.
This software will show how the cafeteria lines change during a lunch period.

Implementation:
I plan to use a std::map to store and manage sinulated students with different status 
across each food station. This map will categorize the students into three std::lists 
representing the student waiting, being served and finished ordering.
The map will have the food-station name as its key. 
The map's value will be an array of these three lists.

Simulated events:
The simulation will focus on three events: 
student be served; student changes lines; a service delay in food-station.
At any given time period, students may be served, change lines, or experience a service delay.
